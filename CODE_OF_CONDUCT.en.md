## Our commitment

As members, contributors, and maintainers of the SOPHENA community, we commit to making participation a respectful, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, experience, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Assume good faith while clarifying disagreements.
- Give and receive feedback concretely and respectfully.
- Protect privacy: do not share other people's stories, data, or screenshots.
- Prioritize what is best for the community and the people who use SOPHENA.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sharing private information without permission, unwanted sexual attention, and conduct that puts others at risk are not tolerated. Issues and Pull Requests must not be used to diagnose, judge, or pressure people who share well-being experiences.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security channel described in [`SECURITY.en.md`](SECURITY.en.md). Include links, context, and relevant evidence; do not publish sensitive information in an Issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality when possible, and may edit, close, or remove content or limit participation temporarily or permanently when needed.
